StreamUx

StreamUx is all about the end users experience. Often video is sent with no consideration to the medium in which it travels. When utilising low bandwidth links often this video can fail. Yes we could adjust at the source, but often the reach back into the source after the fact is not ideal. StreamUx bridges the gap and attempts to capture it at the gateway and push it through at a rate that will just work.
